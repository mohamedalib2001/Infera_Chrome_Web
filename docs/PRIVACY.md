## 2. What the extension handles, and why

The extension is a browsing agent. It carries out the tasks you ask for, in your own browser, and works
only when you are signed in to your Infera Agent account. It never runs in the background without a task
you asked for or scheduled yourself.

| Data | When | Why | Legal basis (UK GDPR art. 6) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your request and the conversation | Every task | To understand and carry out the task | Contract |
| Screenshots, text and structure of the tabs the agent works on; their titles and URLs | While the agent works on them | To see the page and act on it | Contract |
| The list of all open tabs (titles, URLs) | Only when you ask, with "Whole browser" on | To close, sort or take over tabs for you | Contract |
| Console messages and network-request metadata, with authentication values redacted | Only when you ask | To diagnose a page for you | Contract |
| Files you attach | When you attach them | To read them or upload them into a page for you | Contract |
| Account details (name, email, credit balance) via OAuth sign-in | At sign-in | To identify you and charge the work to your credits | Contract |
| Tokens, model and amount of each request, with the task name | Every request | Billing and your cost log | Contract; legal obligation (accounting) |
| Conversations (text and every step, without screenshots or attached files) | Saved in your account unless you turn this off | So your history follows you to every device | Contract (at your request) |
| Long-term memory (up to 2,000 items you asked the agent to remember) | When you or the agent save one | To use your preferences in later tasks | Contract (at your request) |
| Security signals (refused actions, suspected prompt injection) | When they happen | Safety of the service and of your accounts | Legitimate interest |

Memory never stores passwords, one-time codes, card numbers or keys: both the extension and our server
refuse them. Very long tasks have their older steps summarised on the server so the task can continue,
and that summary is saved with the conversation.

## 3. Where the data goes (processors and international transfers)

* **Infera Agent servers:** the extension talks only to inferaagent.com. Our servers run at Hetzner
  Online GmbH in Germany (European Union). Your conversations, memory and cost log are stored there.
* **Anthropic, PBC (United States)** provides the AI models. The content needed for each step of a task
  is sent to it through our servers. Under its commercial terms, API inputs are not used to train its
  models, and they may be kept for a limited time for safety and abuse prevention.

Transfers outside the UK rely on UK adequacy regulations or on appropriate safeguards, such as the UK
International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can
ask privacy@inferaengine.com for details.

## 4. What stays on your device

These are kept in the browser's local storage and deleted when you remove the extension:

* settings and approved sites (signed so they can't be tampered with);
* shortcuts and scheduled tasks;
* a copy of your memory and conversation history.

## 5. How long we keep it

Conversations and memory saved in your account are kept until you delete them, or as set by your
account's data-retention setting. You can do any of these in the extension's Settings:

* delete one conversation;
* delete one memory or all of them;
* stop saving conversations to your account.

When you delete your account, its data is deleted within 30 days and leaves backups as their cycle
expires. Billing records are kept as long as UK accounting and tax law requires.

## 6. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The extension's use of information received from Chrome follows the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the **Limited Use** requirements:

* We use the data only to provide the extension's single purpose (carrying out the browsing tasks you
  ask for) and the features you see.
* We do not sell it.
* We do not use or transfer it for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending decisions, or for any
  purpose unrelated to that single purpose.
* Humans do not read it, except with your consent, for security purposes, or to comply with the law.

## 7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have these rights:

* access your data and get a copy in a portable format;
* have it corrected or erased;
* restrict or object to some processing;
* withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Most of this is in Settings, or write to **privacy@inferaengine.com**; we answer within one month. You
also have the right to complain to the UK **Information Commissioner's Office** (ico.org.uk, 0303 123
1113). We'd appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

The extension makes no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. The agent
acts only on the tasks you give it, and asks you before sensitive actions.

## 8. Security

* Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
* The model provider's key stays on our servers and never reaches the browser.
* Screenshots and attached files are not stored on our servers.
* Payments, purchases, card numbers and identity documents are never handled by the agent.
* Banking and other high-risk sites are blocked by default.
* Text from web pages is treated as untrusted data, never as instructions.

## 9. Children

The service is not directed at people under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## 10. Changes

We will publish any change here and in the service policy, and tell you in the extension or by email
before a material change takes effect.
